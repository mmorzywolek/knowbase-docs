# Leads

When [fallback email collection](behavior.md) is enabled and your assistant can't answer a question, it asks the visitor for their email. These submissions appear in the **Leads** tab.

<!-- screenshot: leads-list -->

## Managing leads

Each lead shows:
- The visitor's email
- The question they asked
- Status: **New**, **Contacted**, or **Resolved**

Click a lead to see details and take action:

- **Reply by email** — opens your email client with the visitor's address
- **View conversation** — see the full chat context
- **Add as Q&A Pair** — create an exact answer so the assistant can handle this question next time

<!-- screenshot: lead-detail-with-actions -->

## Filtering

Filter leads by status:
- **All** — every lead
- **New** — unread leads (with red badge count)
- **Contacted** — leads you've responded to
- **Resolved** — leads that have been handled
