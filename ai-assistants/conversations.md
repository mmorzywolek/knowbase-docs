# Conversations

The Conversations tab shows every chat session visitors have had with your assistant.

<!-- screenshot: conversations-list -->

## Filtering

Filter conversations by:
- **All** — every conversation
- **Positive** — conversations with thumbs up feedback
- **Negative** — conversations with thumbs down feedback
- **Unanswered** — conversations where the assistant couldn't find an answer

## Viewing a conversation

Click any conversation to see the full message thread on the right. Each message shows:
- Whether it's from the visitor or the bot
- Thumbs up/down feedback (if given)
- "Could not answer" badge on messages where the assistant had no relevant context

<!-- screenshot: conversation-detail-view -->

## What to do with this data

- Spot questions your documents don't cover → upload more content or add [Q&A Pairs](creating-an-assistant.md)
- Find negative feedback → improve your system prompt or add guardrails
- Track unanswered questions → add them as Q&A Pairs for exact answers
