# Notion

## Connecting

1. Go to **Account > Connectors**
2. Click **Connect** next to Notion
3. Authorize Knowbase to access your workspace

<!-- screenshot: notion-connect -->

## Using in chat

Toggle the **Notion** icon in the chat input bar. Knowbase searches your Notion pages and includes relevant content in answers.
