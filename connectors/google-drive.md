# Google Drive

## Connecting

1. Go to **Account > Connectors**
2. Click **Connect** next to Google Drive
3. Sign in with your Google account and grant access

<!-- screenshot: google-drive-connect -->

## Using in chat

Once connected, toggle the **Google Drive** icon in the chat input bar. When enabled, Knowbase searches your Drive files alongside your library.

<!-- screenshot: gdrive-toggle-in-chat -->

## Importing files to library

You can also import files from Google Drive directly into your Knowbase library using the file picker in the Upload page.

<!-- screenshot: gdrive-file-picker -->
