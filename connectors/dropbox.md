# Dropbox

## Connecting

1. Go to **Account > Connectors**
2. Click **Connect** next to Dropbox
3. Sign in and authorize access

<!-- screenshot: dropbox-connect -->

## Using in chat

Toggle the **Dropbox** icon in the chat input bar. Knowbase searches your Dropbox files alongside your library.
